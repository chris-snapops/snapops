SnapCalc

SnapCalc is an iOS calculator built to look and behave like a real physical scientific calculator. It uses an arbitrary‑precision math engine under the hood, so results aren't limited by normal floating‑point rounding the way most phone calculators are.

It covers everyday arithmetic, a full set of scientific functions, number base conversion, equation solving, history, memory, and even live currency conversion, all from a physical‑style keypad.
