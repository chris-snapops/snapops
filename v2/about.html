About SnapOps

Run by Chris Becher, SnapOps is a sales operations and revenue operations consultancy built around one idea: your process should be snappy, not sluggish.

Why “SnapOps”

Most operational problems inside a revenue team aren't a strategy problem, they're a process problem. A deal stalls because the CRM stage doesn't match reality. An integration breaks quietly and nobody notices until data stops flowing between systems. A report takes an hour to build by hand every week. SnapOps exists to fix that kind of friction, quickly and directly, whether the process in question belongs to sales, service, integrations, or reporting.

I work as an independent consultant, which means every engagement is scoped, quoted, and delivered by me directly, rather than handed off between account managers and delivery staff.

How I work with clients

24 hr response time

You shouldn't have to wait days to hear back. I respond within 1 business day, every time.

Pay for completion

You're paying for the project to be finished, not for my hours. If it takes longer than expected, that's on me.

Clear communication

After every meeting, you get a recap by email: what I'm doing next, what's needed from you, and where the timeline stands.

A defined process

Every engagement starts with a free scoping call and a written quote, runs over 2-6 weeks of implementation, and ends with two weeks of post‑project support.
